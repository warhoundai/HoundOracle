# Redundant-head ablation — rejected

Campaign19's encoder validation cross-entropy was lower before than after the added logistic correction (.590 versus.618). This motivated a separate, preregistered identity-head experiment. The19 training failure remains unchanged; no test labels were used to propose the ablation.

The fixed identity matrix forwards exactly the original three NLI logits and ignores both contextual scalars. Compare stable1921+learned correction,1921+identity and1935+identity on the same207 development queries; correctness maps fit only on the existing1,000 fit queries. No weight training, threshold search or cloud charges.

| Configuration | Raw accuracy | Provisional coverage | Correct-definitive change vs stable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Stable1921+logistic |77.78%|41.06%|reference|
|1921+identity |78.26%|39.61%|−0.97points|
|1935+identity |76.81%|38.65%|−1.93points|

Both identity variants fail the material-gain/coverage screen. **Reject**. Neither fresh risk nor locked600 labels were consumed. This shows that better cross-entropy alone did not translate to the requested useful-answer benefit. Preserve the stable research checkpoint; no public/default architecture replacement or broad superiority claim.
